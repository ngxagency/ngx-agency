Privacy

How NGX Labs handles information on this site. Effective November 27, 2026.

Who we are

NGX Labs is an operations firm led by Joey Ngoy. This policy covers ngx.agency and the free audit on this site. The only contact address we publish is hello@ngx.agency.

Information we collect

If you request a free audit, we collect the fields you submit: name, work email, company, website (if you provide one), staff size, role, admin hours per week, and an optional message. We do not ask for a phone number or a street address.

If you arrive from a campaign link, we store these parameters in session storage for the visit, under the key ngx_utm_payload, and attach them if you submit the form: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term, fbclid, gclid, and li_fat_id, plus the landing path.

Server logs may include the pages you request, the time, the browser type, the referring address, and an IP address, used to keep the site reliable and secure.

How we use it

We use audit submissions to prepare the audit, reply to you, and decide whether an engagement is a fit. We use campaign parameters to see which visits lead to a request. We do not sell personal information.

Measurement tools

This preview ships with empty IDs. A tool loads only after both are true: its ID is filled in, and you choose Allow on the consent bar. A browser Global Privacy Control signal keeps every tool off. The tools we may enable later are:

  • Meta Pixel, for page views and audit submissions from Meta campaigns.
  • TikTok Pixel, for page views and audit submissions from TikTok campaigns.
  • Google Analytics 4, for site measurement.
  • Google Ads conversion, using a conversion ID and label, for ad measurement.
  • Microsoft Clarity, for aggregated session measurement.
  • Google Search Console verification, a meta tag that confirms we operate the site. It is inserted only when its ID is set and consent is granted.

Your choice is stored in local storage under ngx_consent. Decline, or leave the bar unanswered, and none of these tools load. We do not send health or payment data through them.

How long we keep it

We keep audit details only as long as we need them to reply, to run an engagement you ask for, or to meet a legal duty. Campaign parameters in the browser last for the session.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, including California under the CCPA and CPRA, you can ask us to tell you what we hold, to correct it, to delete it, and to stop sharing it for cross-context advertising. Email hello@ngx.agency with the subject Privacy Request. We will not treat you differently for asking.

Children

This site is for businesses. It is not for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date above.
